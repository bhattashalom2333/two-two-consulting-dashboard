Data scope and limitations

The retail file contains 15,000 observations across eight product categories and six regions. It does not identify individual products or SKUs. Customer metrics use 18,000 records, and operational metrics use 12,000 supply chain records. The files do not share a transaction, customer, or supplier key, so the analysis cannot claim that a specific promotion caused a customer to churn. The $18–25M lost-profit opportunity comes from HRG leadership in the case packet. Recovery scenarios remain planning hypotheses until controlled tests and Finance reconciliation verify them.
