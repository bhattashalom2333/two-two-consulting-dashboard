Data scope and limitations

Retail metrics use 15,000 observations. Customer metrics use 18,000 records. Operational metrics use 12,000 supply-chain records. The files do not share a transaction, customer, or supplier key, so the analysis connects findings at an aggregate level. Promotion ROI equals profit divided by estimated discount investment. Projections are planning scenarios that require controlled-pilot verification and Finance approval.
