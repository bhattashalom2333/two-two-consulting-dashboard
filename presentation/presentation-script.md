Good morning. We will start with the outcome HRG wants, show the dashboard in one minute, and then walk through the evidence, the operating plan, and the decisions required today.

The base case adds $12 million in verified annual profit. That moves projected profit from $38.4 million to $50.4 million and margin from 9.9% to 13.0%, assuming revenue stays constant.

Finance books the value only after the evidence clears the agreed threshold.

## Slide 4: Why this matters

Revenue growth reached 12.7% in 2025 while profit growth fell to negative 4.8%. Leadership estimates annual profit leakage at $18 million to $25 million.

That gap explains the urgency.

## Slide 5: Priority choice

HRG has seven problem areas. The Q1 and Q2 plan concentrates on promotion margin, loss-making sales, churn risk, and customer value because the current data supports immediate owners and controlled tests.

Slow inventory, supply chain, and logistics move into Q3 after Finance reconciles the operating baseline. The dashboard addresses the reporting gap now.

## Slide 6: The Q1 and Q2 solution

The dashboard turns the four priority problems into four decisions: install promotion guardrails, assign loss pockets, protect high-value customers at risk, and replace loyalty-tier targeting with customer value and risk.

Together, those priorities support the $12 million base case.

## Slide 7: Promotion evidence

BOGO margin is negative 16.4%, and Clearance margin is negative 35.6%. Neither produces a measurable unit lift.

HRG should require a margin floor, a campaign ROI threshold, a holdout group, and a stop rule before funding a deep offer.

## Slide 8: Customer decision

The dashboard identifies 1,408 high-value customers at the greatest modeled risk.

The first pilot uses a capped 10% offer, a 15% untreated comparison group, and a 90-day retained-value readout. The other groups receive different treatment based on their value and risk.

## Slide 9: Profit and margin scenarios

The base case starts with $18 million of gross benefit and subtracts $6 million of implementation cost. The result is $12 million of net annual profit.

These figures are planning scenarios. Finance requires a reconciled baseline, a valid comparison, all costs, and two positive reads before annualizing value.

## Slide 10: 180-day action plan

HRG controls leakage first, runs two narrow pilots, proves incremental value, and scales only the rules that clear the evidence gate.

Q2 also prepares the operating cost baseline for the Q3 supply chain and logistics workstream.

## Slide 11: Board decision

We need three decisions today: approve the promotion guardrails, name one executive value owner, and fund the dashboard and two controlled pilots within the $6 million envelope.

The Q3 operating case returns after Finance completes the reconciliation.
