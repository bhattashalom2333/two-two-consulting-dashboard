# Two Two Consulting pitch script

## Slide 1: HRG profit recovery plan

Good morning. We want to start with where HRG wants to end: more profit from the growth the business already has.

Our recommendation concentrates on four problems that HRG can test in Q1 and Q2. The work is supported by a live dashboard and a 180-day action plan.

## Slide 2: Proposed destination

The base case is $12 million in verified annual profit uplift. That moves projected profit from $38.4 million to $50.4 million and margin from 9.9% to 13.0%, assuming revenue stays constant.

This is a planning case. Finance records the value only after HRG has a valid baseline, a holdout comparison, all costs, and two positive measurement cycles.

## Slide 3: One-minute dashboard walkthrough

I will show the dashboard in four clicks.

First, I choose one of the four priority problems. Second, I change the region. Third, I test either a promotion or a customer segment. The final view gives us the owner, the next action, and the threshold for success.

During the live demo:

1. Select Promotion margin and point out the negative BOGO and Clearance margins.
2. Select Loss-making sales, change the region to West, and show the 53.4% loss-making share.
3. Open the promotion test and move the discount or expected lift slider.
4. Open the action plan and select the recommended four actions. The working plan should show $12.0 million in annual value.

## Slide 4: Priority choice

HRG has seven problem areas. We are concentrating the pitch on promotion margin, loss-making sales, churn risk, and customer value because the current data supports immediate owners and controlled tests.

Fragmented reporting enables the work through the dashboard. Inventory, supply chain, and logistics return in Q3 after Finance reconciles the operating cost baseline.

## Slide 5: Why now

Revenue growth reached 12.7% in 2025 while profit growth fell to negative 4.8%. Leadership estimates that HRG loses $18 million to $25 million in profit each year.

That gap is the reason to act now.

## Slide 6: Promotion margin

No Promotion produces a 23.4% margin. BOGO falls to negative 16.4%, and Clearance falls to negative 35.6%.

The units chart matters because it does not show a measurable lift from those deeper offers. HRG should require a 5% margin floor, 50% campaign ROI, a holdout group, and a stop rule before scaling a promotion.

## Slide 7: Loss-making sales

HRG is profitable overall, but 54.5% of the observations lose money. Labor remains close to $23 million in each sales-size group even though revenue changes sharply.

The action is a weekly file that ranks the top 50 recurring loss pockets by dollar loss and frequency. Each of the top pockets gets one owner and one recovery lever.

## Slide 8: Customer value and churn

The top quarter of customers holds 55% of customer lifetime value. Loyalty tiers do not separate that value well, and Bronze customers have the highest observed churn rate.

HRG should pair customer value with risk before spending another retention dollar.

## Slide 9: Value by Risk solution

The Value by Risk view identifies 1,408 Protect-now customers. The first retention test uses a narrow audience, a capped 10% offer, and a randomized 15% holdout.

For Grow-and-retain customers, HRG should test service and relevant engagement before using a discount.

## Slide 10: Solution controls

The models help HRG choose who and what to test. They do not prove that the intervention created value.

The churn model ranks outreach. The profit model tests promotion rules. A holdout group and Finance reconciliation determine the realized result.

## Slide 11: 180-day action plan

The first 30 days control leakage and establish the baseline. Days 31 to 60 run the promotion and retention pilots. Days 61 to 120 measure the result and close the next loss pockets. The final phase scales only the rules that cleared the threshold.

If a test misses its threshold, HRG fixes the mechanism before adding scale.

## Slide 12: Q3 follow-on

Supply chain and logistics remain in scope. The operational file has a $449.7 million cost base and no transaction key linking it to retail activity.

Finance reconciles that baseline in Q2. HRG then opens the supplier, inventory, and logistics workstream in Q3.

## Slide 13: Board decision

We are asking for three decisions today.

Approve the promotion guardrails. Name one executive value owner. Fund the dashboard and two controlled pilots within the $6 million implementation envelope.

The Q3 operating case returns after Finance completes the reconciliation.
