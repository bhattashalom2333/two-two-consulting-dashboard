## Short answers for likely questions

### What is a loss-making combination?

A combination of category, region, store type, and promotion that repeatedly produces negative profit in the supplied retail sample. For example, West, Seasonal, a specific store type, and BOGO could form one combination.

### What does "publish the top 50" mean?

FP&A issues a weekly ranked list of the 50 combinations with the largest or most frequent losses. The list includes an owner, the proposed corrective action, a due date, and the result at the next review.

### What is promotion margin?

Total profit divided by total revenue for the selected promotion records. In this file, profit equals revenue minus cost of goods sold and labor cost.

### What is a margin floor?

A minimum result that a pilot must achieve before HRG expands it. The dashboard proposes 5 percent for the pilot. Finance should decide the final scale threshold.

### Does a five-point lift guarantee more customers?

No. It is an assumption for planning. If baseline conversion is 12 percent, a five-point lift means the model assumes 17 percent conversion. A holdout group measures whether the offer actually caused an increase.

### Why are supply chain and logistics scheduled for Q3?

The supply file reports $449.7 million in combined holding and transportation cost, but that sample total has not been reconciled to the ledger. Finance completes the reconciliation in Q2, then HRG can build a credible Q3 operating case.
